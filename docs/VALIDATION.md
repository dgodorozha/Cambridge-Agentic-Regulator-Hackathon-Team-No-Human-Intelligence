# Validation notes (model card)

## What HSL is

A pre certification stress test for surveillance sentinels and intervention
rules on synthetic AI agent markets. Its outputs are comparative rankings of
tools and rules on a declared battery, with intervals. They are not
forecasts of any real market and never recommend live intervention.

## Data

Synthetic only. Markets are generated by an agent based simulator with
noise traders, fundamentalists, vendor clusters with parameterised signal
correlation and share, and, in some families, independently trained RL
traders. No personal data, no venue data. Every scenario is reproducible
from `battery.json` (seeds included).

## Ground truth

Destabilising agents are defined generatively: members of a vendor cluster
whose signal correlation and share exceed declared thresholds (0.6 and
10%), and RL agents whose learned policies converged and amplified the
shock. Sentinels never see these labels. The labels are a modelling choice
and the briefing says so.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Decision F1 | F1 of flagged agents against ground truth on herding scenarios | does not penalise quiet market alerts; read with the false alert rate and the budget |
| Aggregate fidelity (conventional) | correlation of the stress index with rolling volatility | identical to the volatility trigger's output; scores it 1.00 by construction |
| Aggregate fidelity (multi moment) | mean correlation with rolling volatility, absolute return and mean pairwise flow correlation | no tool is scored against its own output |
| Decision gap | normalised Kendall tau distance between the two rankings | with k tools tau takes few values; quote P(inversion) from the bootstrap |
| P(inversion) | share of scenario resamples in which the rankings invert | resamples scenarios, not families; with 22 calibration scenarios the resolution is coarse |
| Lead time | steps from first alert to the shock | structural detection latency, not a forecast |
| Containment | one minus treated over untreated mean post shock drawdown | path dependent; the trough measure and the whole path measure are also reported |
| Dislocation probability | share of herding scenarios with post shock drawdown above 0.10 | threshold is declared, not estimated |
| Burden by class | share of intended order flow suppressed after the shock, by class and by ground truth | the cost side; a targeted rule that suppresses fundamentalists is removing the stabilisers |
| Autonomy breadth | share of scenarios in the backed score tails | in sample by construction; the leave one out claim rate is the figure to quote |
| Evidence grade | A, B, C from scenario count and interval width | a summary of precision, not of validity |

## Version 3 assurance layer

- **Two ground truths.** The generative truth (clusters above the correlation
  and share thresholds, manipulators, large momentum following personas) is
  what decision F1 scores. The behavioural truth is the Shapley value of post
  shock drawdown by counterfactual silencing under common random numbers;
  each sentinel is also graded on the share of its flags that land on the
  highest impact group. Where the two disagree the briefing shows both.
- **Guarantee, not estimate.** The false alert budget is enforced by a
  conformal threshold on the eight quiet calibration scenarios. The
  guarantee is a statement about exchangeable quiet scenarios; the held out
  quiet family is a different population and its alert rate is an empirical
  check, reported next to the bound and not as a guaranteed quantity.
- **Truth dependence.** A reduced battery is re run under imitation herding
  and square root impact and the sentinels are re ranked. The briefing
  states whether rank one survives; a certification that depends on the
  generator is reported as conditional.
- **Adversarial worst case.** The certified sentinel's F1 under the worst
  evasion found by a budgeted random search, subject to the market still
  dislocating. The search is seeded and bounded; a larger budget or a
  learned attacker (adaptive stress testing proper) would find worse cases,
  so the reported margin is an upper bound on robustness, not a lower bound.
- **Injection suite.** Eleven cases against the model facing surfaces, run
  on every battery. They cover the attacks we thought of; a benchmark such
  as AgentDojo would cover more.
- **Thresholds.** The three herding sentinels were calibrated on the
  demonstration quiet family, like the four core sentinels; the conformal
  layer reports what that calibration is worth and the held out rows show
  where it does not carry.
- **Dose response.** Two seeds per share level; the curvature sign and the
  threshold share are descriptive, and the sweep tests a prediction inside
  the sandbox, not in a market.

## Known limitations

- Thresholds for all sentinels and rules were calibrated on the
  demonstration families. The held out family shows where calibration does
  not carry; on the current battery the absorption ratio's false alert rate
  rises on the held out quiet family.
- Targeted rules act on flags observed on the untreated path. There is no
  lookahead in time, but the sentinel does not re observe the treated market.
- Fundamentalists are as mutually correlated as a herd because they share a
  signal; correlation based sentinels flag them, and targeted rules then
  suppress the stabilising force. This is a property of correlation based
  surveillance the battery exposes, not a bug in the battery.
- Every rule engaged before the shock on most herd scenarios because the
  herd's own swings trip it. Whether intervening on endogenous herd
  volatility is desirable is a policy question.
- Bootstrap intervals treat scenarios as exchangeable; scenarios within a
  family share parameters, so intervals are optimistic for family level
  claims.

## Version 3 additions to the limitations

- Jurisdiction rules are stylised after public mechanisms with durations in
  simulation steps; they compare regimes, they do not reproduce any venue's
  calibration.
- Offline persona tables are documented synthetic surrogates. A model
  distilled table is a snapshot of one model on one day, hashed so that a
  replay can detect a change but not so that it can reproduce the model.
- The Hawkes branching ratio is estimated on threshold exceedances over a
  rolling window with a profiled exponential kernel; Hardiman, Bercot and
  Bouchaud (2013) show that such estimates are sensitive to the kernel and
  the window, so the sentinel's stress index is a comparative signal.

## Reproducibility

`artefacts.json` records the code fingerprint, library versions, battery
hash, seeds, bootstrap size and elapsed time. The CLI replays a battery from
an evidence pack. The unit tests pin the v1 dynamics so future changes to
the simulator are detected.
